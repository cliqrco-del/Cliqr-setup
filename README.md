# Cliqr-setup
A free voice assistant for your Mac that lives in the notch. Hold Control + Option and talk: Cliqr sees your screen, clicks and types for you, teaches on a whiteboard, joins your Google Meet, takes meeting notes, makes PDFs and shows your weather and music. Private by design, with no accounts or tracking. Bring your own free Gemini key.
